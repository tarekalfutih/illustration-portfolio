Hello and welcome to my portfolio!

I’m an illustrator and designer based in Gothenburg, Sweden. I’ve been illustrating children’s books since 2008, with more than 20 published titles.

I also hold a Master’s degree in Interaction Design from Chalmers University of Technology. Combining illustration with UX design allows me to create engaging, meaningful, and user-friendly experiences.

You can explore my UX and Product Design work at tarekdesign.se.
